Build the Board

Pick a theme and set up your teams to get started. No dedicated host needed — everyone plays from the same screen.

❓ How does this work?
  1. Pick a theme and add your teams below, then tap Start Quiz.
  2. Whoever's turn it is calls out a category and point value shown in the turn banner, and taps that tile.
  3. Read the question out loud. The answer stays hidden until someone taps Show Answer — or the timer runs out in Automated mode.
  4. Any team can tap their own +points button once they've answered correctly.
  5. The turn passes automatically to the next team. When the board is empty, the final results appear on their own.

Set Up the Round

Choose a theme, add your players, and pass the device around. No dedicated host needed — whoever's holding the phone just taps through their own turn.

❓ How does this work?
  1. Pick a theme and player count below, then tap Deal the Words.
  2. The device gets passed around — each player privately taps to reveal their word, then hides it before passing it on.
  3. Almost everyone gets the same word. One random player — the Spy — gets a different, related word.
  4. Once everyone's seen their word, the group discusses out loud and tries to spot who's bluffing.
  5. Anyone can tap Reveal the Spy when the group is ready to vote.

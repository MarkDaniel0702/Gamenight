❓ How does this work?
  1. Pick a category, add your players, then tap Start.
  2. Each turn, one player privately sees a secret word and gives one-word clues out loud — no rhyming, no spelling.
  3. The rest of the group shouts guesses. Tap They Got It! the moment someone's right.
  4. Fewer clues used means more points — the app scores it for you.
  5. The clue-giver role rotates automatically every round.

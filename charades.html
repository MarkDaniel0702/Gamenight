Charades

No host needed — the app deals the word, runs the clock, and passes the turn automatically.

❓ How does this work?
  1. Pick a category and add your players, then tap Start.
  2. Each turn, one player privately sees a secret word or phrase and acts it out — no talking!
  3. The group shouts out guesses. Anyone taps Correct! the moment it's guessed.
  4. If time runs out first, tap Skip and the next player goes.
  5. Correct rounds score a point for the actor — totally optional, just for fun.

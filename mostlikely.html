Who's Most Likely?

No host needed — everyone votes for someone in the group, the app tallies it up.

❓ How does this work?
  1. Pick a category and add your players (at least 3), then tap Start.
  2. A question appears, like "Most likely to become famous?" Each player taps who they'd vote for — in turn.
  3. Once everyone's voted, the results reveal automatically, ranked by votes.
  4. Tap Next Question to keep going, or Skip anytime.

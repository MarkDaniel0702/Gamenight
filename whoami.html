Who Am I?

No host needed — the app assigns everyone a secret identity and keeps time automatically.

❓ How does this work?
  1. Pick a category and add your players, then tap Start.
  2. Each player gets a secret character — held up for the group to see, not the player themselves.
  3. The player asks yes-or-no questions out loud to guess who they are, while the group answers.
  4. Tap Got It! when they guess right, or let the timer run out.
  5. The turn passes automatically to the next player.

Two Truths and a Lie

No host needed — the turn, the statements, and the voting all rotate through the app automatically.

❓ How does this work?
  1. Add your players, then tap Start.
  2. Each turn, one player either writes 3 statements about themselves (2 true, 1 false) or grabs a ready-made prompt.
  3. Everyone reads the 3 statements — only the storyteller knows which is the lie.
  4. Every other player votes on which one they think is false.
  5. The app reveals the answer and tallies who guessed right, then passes the turn to the next player.

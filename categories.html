Categories

No host needed — the app picks the category, keeps time, and moves the turn along.

❓ How does this work?
  1. Add your players and tap Start — a random category appears.
  2. Going in turn order, each player names something in that category before the timer runs out.
  3. Tap Nailed It! once they say something, or Stuck / Repeated if they can't — the group self-referees, the app just keeps the pace.
  4. In Elimination Mode, getting stuck knocks you out — last player standing wins. Turn it off for a casual, no-pressure round.
